YancoTV support

Last updated: 17 November 2026

YancoTV plays the IPTV service you already subscribe to. It does not provide channels, playlists or subscriptions, so the answers below are about the app; for anything about your channels or your account, your provider is the one to ask.

Contact

Email contact@yancoverse.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened. In the app, Settings → About → Copy details for a bug report copies your app version and device model, which helps us find the problem faster. Please do not send your provider password; we never need it.

Adding your provider

Open Settings → Sources and tap Add. Choose the type your provider gave you: an M3U playlist link, an M3U file, an Xtream account (server address, username and password) or a Stalker portal (portal address and MAC address). Then tap Add & sync. A large catalogue can take a few minutes to read, and you can keep using the app while it does.

Your username and password are encrypted on your device and are sent only to your provider.

Common questions

A channel or film will not play. Many providers allow only one stream at a time on an account, and some keep a stopped stream counted for a minute or two. Stop playback on your other devices, wait a moment, and try again. If nothing plays at all, check that your subscription is still active; Settings → Sources shows when it expires, where your provider reports it.

The programme guide is empty. The guide comes from your provider. Xtream accounts usually include one; many M3U playlists do not. To download it again, tap Settings → Programme guide.

Films or series seem to be missing. Open Settings → Sources, tap Sync on the source, and then Verify catalogue. It compares what your provider lists with what the app holds and tells you if anything is missing.

Some categories are not shown. Categories marked as adult are hidden until you choose otherwise, and you can hide or lock others yourself. Both are in Settings → Privacy → Parental controls.

Recording stops when I leave the app. A recording follows the channel you are watching. To keep it going with the screen off or in another app, turn on Settings → Keep recording in the background. It uses more battery.

My TV does not appear when I cast. YancoTV casts to Chromecast and Google Cast TVs on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. If iOS asked whether YancoTV may find devices on your network and you said no, turn it on in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Moving to a new phone. In Settings → Backup, tap Save a backup, then use Restore on the new device. Set a password when you save it: it encrypts your source settings. With many providers the stream addresses saved in a backup still contain your login, so keep the file somewhere private.

Crash reports. When the app crashes or freezes it sends a report so the fault can be fixed. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Crash reports. What a report contains is set out in the privacy policy.
